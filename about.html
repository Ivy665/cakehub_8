Orange cake

A bright and refreshing orange cake made with fresh citrus juice and aromatic zest.
It offers a soft, moist texture with a balance of sweetness and tangy citrus flavor,
finished with a smooth cream frosting and delicate decorations.

Macaron Cake

A macaron cake is a giant version of a French macaron or a dessert built by stacking many small macarons together.
It has a crisp outer crust, a soft and chewy center, and rich fillings like buttercream, ganache, or fresh fruit

Chocolate Cake

Chocolate cake is a rich, sweet baked dessert flavored with melted chocolate or cocoa powder.
It features a soft, tender crumb, a deep brown color, and a sweet taste. People top it with creamy buttercream, rich fudge, or glossy ganache.

Pink Cake

A fun and playful celebration cake coated in pastel pink frosting with a vibrant drip effect.
Topped with colorful sprinkles and a playful upside-down ice cream cone, it features a light,fluffy sponge with sweet berry notes
